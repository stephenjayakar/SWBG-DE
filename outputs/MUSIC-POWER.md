# Music and power repair

The music port uses the installed SWGB/Expanding Fronts files. The original has faction-specific short cues in Sound/Stream/ef_civ1.mp3 (Empire) and ef_civ3.mp3 (Rebels). EF's dllmain.cpp installs the stream/ef_civ%d.mp3 pattern at executable addresses 0x5174AF and 0x51B2CC; these are civilization-selection cues. The source game-start music routine at 0x5EDD90 chooses the shared CD/stream tracks without a civilization lookup. The two installed MUSIC files total 130 minutes, 16 seconds. There is no evidence here of separate full background playlists for Empire and Rebels.

The converted companion supplies GalacticEmpire_theme.wem and RebelAlliance_theme.wem using DE's exact internal civilization names. This moves the original selection cue to DE's match-opening theme. Shared music fills all 30 documented ingame slots, in contiguous portions of Track02 followed by Track03, with every decoded PCM frame preserved. These cuts are not original song boundaries. DE's **Standard** soundtrack playlist preserves slot order and plays the faction theme; Classic omits the theme, and shuffle modes change the order. Transitions, save/load and actual playback require in-game verification. The EF Yavin menu theme also supplies the lobby; source credits, victory and defeat tracks are included. Music volume uses DE's normal music control. Disable conflicting music mods if they take priority.

Audio conversion uses Wwise PCM containers, decoded and compared with vgmstream. The 38 new files add roughly 1.4 GB of uncompressed music. They install under the existing asset companion, not into Steam. No online publication is performed.

Power investigation found a concrete upgrade bug: the old adapter only gave an aura to core 12 (TL1). Age technologies replace it with 498 (TL2), 132 (TL3), or 20 (TL4), none of which had an aura. The repair follows the entire source upgrade family, including four evacuation variants. It adds no aura to unrelated buildings. Source class 18 is the broad building class and must not be used to select only cores.

The adapter now derives its task from the native work-rate aura on building 2275. Work-rate aura amounts are proportional bonuses: native 0.1 means +10%; a +3 bonus restores 0.25 base rate to 1.0. The prior movement-aura template used a factor of 4, which was the wrong encoding for this attribute. One task per emitter enables the visible radius bit, avoiding overlapping copies of the same ring. Core radius remains 10 tiles; mobile power droids use 5.

Test a **fresh match** after restarting DE. Turn **Building and Aura Range Indicator** on in game options. With no aegis cheat, train the same unit in Troop Centers inside and outside core range at TL1 and again after advancing to TL2/TL3/TL4; test War Center research similarly. Unpowered duration should be four times powered duration. Two overlapping cores should not increase the bonus further; destroying the last nearby core should slow production again. Check the placement preview and selected completed core for the range indicator. Those runtime behaviors, overlap and boundary rules remain unverified. Command Centers are power-exempt. Garrisoned droids are still unimplemented.
